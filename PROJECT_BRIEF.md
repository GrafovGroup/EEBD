Updated November 15, 2026. The owner authorized and completed publication of a full bilingual enquiry website at https://eebd.ca/ on existing WHC hosting. Operator: Superclean Canada Inc.; contact: info@eebd.ca. Approved identity: Precision 01 with the descriptor directly under EEBD and no .ca in the logo.

Buyers are vessel operators, fleet procurement teams, ship-service companies and selected industrial facilities. The website helps them communicate equipment requirements and separately scoped training or service requests. General contact, equipment, training and service journeys work in English and French with validation, review/edit and Formspree delivery.

Launch scope is a complete enquiry website, not a stocked catalogue. Suppliers, trainers and service partners are not arranged; public copy says so. Individual models require exact variant, supply, media, approval, safety-notice and service evidence before publication. No catalogue is inferred from manufacturer research.

Canada-wide enquiries are accepted. Equipment delivery, instructor availability and service coverage must each be confirmed by request. No local premises, national field capacity, public pricing, checkout, rentals, automated equipment-selection advice, rescue/firefighting suitability, invented credentials, service certificates or blanket replacement intervals are asserted.

The live release includes 84 pages across 42 paired purposes, bilingual privacy information, the approved branding, HTTPS, custom error handling and a verified form notification route.
