Final font selection/licensing and path conversion for reproducible third-party printing remain part of the selected identity's refinement.

Provisional clear space: at least half the letter height around a full lockup. Retain the descriptor in header/footer versions. Compact favicon marks are the intentional tiny-size exception. Inspect at actual size before approving. Current favicon assets are provisional and must be regenerated for the selected identity; they are not proof of approval.
