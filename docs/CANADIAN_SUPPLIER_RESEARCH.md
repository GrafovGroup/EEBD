# Canadian EEBD supplier research

Research date: November 16, 2026. Prepared for Superclean Canada Inc. / EEBD.ca using manufacturer and supplier websites. This is an internal prospect shortlist. No supplier was contacted, no application was submitted, and no partnership, resale permission, stock, pricing or delivery commitment has been established.

## Recommended first channels

The following order is an assessment of the evidence below, not a statement that any company has agreed to work with EEBD.ca:

1. **Survitec:** strongest initial marine enquiry because its catalogue lists two relevant EEBD families, Ocenco identifies it as an authorized maritime distributor, and its own directory lists Canadian locations on both coasts.
2. **Dräger Safety Canada:** strongest manufacturer-direct business-development route because it publishes a channel-partner application and can clarify current Canadian Saver configurations and documentation.
3. **VIKING Life-Saving Equipment:** another manufacturer-authorized maritime channel, with separately listed operational and training Ocenco models. Canadian fulfilment and the EEBD scope of its Canadian service network need confirmation.

Pacific Coast Liferaft is a useful Vancouver Island retail lead. Levitt-Safety is more relevant to a separate mining/industrial sourcing discussion. SupplyNow is an Ontario retail sourcing lead. Neither an online listing nor an ordinary business account establishes wholesale rights.

### 1. Survitec — marine supply candidate with both-coast presence

Survitec publishes product pages for the [Ocenco M20.2 EEBD](https://survitecgroup.com/personal-protection/breathing-air-equipment/ocenco-m202-eebd) and [Spiroscape EEBD](https://survitecgroup.com/personal-protection/breathing-air-equipment/spiroscape-eebd). Ocenco lists Survitec under its authorized maritime and offshore oil distribution channel. The directory supplies **info@survitecgroup.com** as the general contact. [Ocenco distributor directory](https://www.ocenco.com/distributors/).

Survitec’s directory lists **1689 Cliveden Avenue, Delta, BC**, telephone **604-214-4314**, **vancouver.service@survitecgroup.com**, and a Survitec / DBC Marine Safety Systems location at **27 Nils Way, St. John’s, NL**, telephone **709-747-2175**. Both entries include fire and personal protection among broader service categories. These are useful regional contact points; the directory does not establish which EEBD models each branch stocks or is authorized to service. [Survitec service-station directory](https://survitecgroup.com/service-stations).

**Commercial enquiry to resolve:** Canadian dealer/reseller eligibility; the correct sales entity; model-specific supply, warranty and service responsibilities; Vancouver versus Newfoundland fulfilment; and access to training units, mounting accessories and approved product media. A published retail or trade price schedule for EEBD.ca was not established.

### 2. Dräger Safety Canada — manufacturer and formal channel application

The Canadian entity is listed at **2425 Skymark Avenue, Unit 1, Mississauga, ON L4W 4Y6**, telephone **1-877-372-4371**. Its [channel-partner application](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_ca/Safety/Join-Portal) explicitly invites prospective partners and requests company, geography, market segment and business-experience information. This is a concrete route for a possible reseller relationship; acceptance and commercial terms remain unknown. A separate [safety quote request](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_ca/Request-Quote-Safety) is available for product enquiries.

The Canadian product page identifies the **Saver CF N (NIOSH)** family. At the research date it describes 5-, 10- and 15-minute variants but explicitly states that the **15-minute version is temporarily unavailable**. Treat this as a dated manufacturer statement requiring reconfirmation, not an inventory report for all distributors. The page’s family-level approval wording should not replace confirmation of the exact Canadian part number, configuration and current documents. [Dräger Saver CF N product page](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_ca/Products/Saver-CF-N-NIOSH).

**Commercial enquiry to resolve:** whether Dräger will appoint EEBD.ca directly or refer it to an existing distributor; permitted geography and markets; current orderable variants; and responsibility for training, inspection, warranty and safety notices.

### 3. VIKING Life-Saving Equipment — authorized maritime channel

Ocenco identifies VIKING as an authorized maritime/offshore distributor and publishes **viking@viking-life.com** as its contact. [Ocenco distributor directory](https://www.ocenco.com/distributors/). VIKING separately lists the [Ocenco M-20.2 EEBD, item 1008018](https://www.viking-life.com/products/firefighting-equipment/breathing-and-respiratory/eebds/eebd-m-202-ocenco/) and the [M-20.2T training model, item 1008021](https://www.viking-life.com/products/firefighting-equipment/breathing-and-respiratory/eebds/eebd-training-model-m-202t-ocenco/). The training model must remain a distinct product in any future catalogue.

Its service-network directory contains Canadian entries, including Vancouver Liferaft & Safety in North Vancouver and K & D Pratt Group in Mount Pearl, Newfoundland and Labrador. These entries establish a Canadian network presence; they do **not** establish that those companies distribute or service Ocenco EEBDs. No current VIKING-owned Canadian office was verified in this review. [VIKING service network](https://www.viking-life.com/service/find-service-station/).

The [headquarters contact page](https://www.viking-life.com/contact/headquarters/) provides an enquiry route. **Commercial enquiry to resolve:** the designated Canadian sales/fulfilment entity, eligibility for resale, local EEBD service authorization, operational/training bundles, and warranty handling. Manufacturer distributor status does not automatically make EEBD.ca an authorized subdealer.

### 4. Pacific Coast Liferaft — Vancouver Island retail lead

Pacific Coast Liferaft lists an **Ocenco M20.2** in its online store. Its published address is **2-956 Devonshire Road, Victoria, BC V9A 4T8**; contact **info@liferaft.ca**, **250-360-1573**. The listing includes TC/SOLAS wording, which should be checked against the exact supplied unit’s documentation before reuse in an EEBD.ca offer. [Pacific Coast Liferaft product category and contact details](https://www.liferaft.ca/store/oceanco/).

The reviewed evidence supports a Canadian marine retailer carrying the model. It does not establish an Ocenco-authorized distributorship, a wholesale programme or a commitment to supply EEBD.ca. Any authorization concerning liferafts or marine evacuation systems would not by itself prove EEBD authorization.

**Commercial enquiry to resolve:** trade pricing and resale permission, direct versus upstream warranty handling, unit manufacture dates and documentation, mounting accessories, and freight options for Vancouver Island and mainland customers. Coastal proximity makes this a practical lead; stock and delivery dates still require a specific quote.

### 5. Levitt-Safety — authorized Canadian mining channel

Ocenco lists Levitt-Safety under **Canada Mining Market**, with **csr@levitt-safety.com**. This is explicit manufacturer evidence of a market-specific authorized distribution relationship. It is not evidence of a marine M-20.2 appointment. [Ocenco distributor directory](https://www.ocenco.com/distributors/).

Levitt-Safety’s catalogue lists the **Ocenco M-20 Self-Contained Self Rescuer**, seller part **MO930065**. The listing should not be relabelled as the marine M-20.2. [Levitt-Safety M-20 product page](https://www.levitt-safety.com/Product/MO930065). The company’s [location directory](https://www.levitt-safety.com/company/location) includes Port Coquitlam, BC; Moncton, NB; and Saint-Laurent in the Montréal area, among other Canadian branches. The general telephone is **1-888-453-8488**.

**Commercial enquiry to resolve:** which industrial/mining products and markets it could support, whether it sells for onward resale, exact model/configuration, and which branches handle product-specific service and training. A national branch network is useful contact coverage, not proof that a particular unit or specialist is available locally.

### 6. SupplyNow Limited — Ontario retail/B2B sourcing lead

SupplyNow lists [Dräger Saver CF5 (N), part 4042055](https://www.supplynow.ca/products/draeger-4042055), and [Saver CF10 (N), part 4042056](https://www.supplynow.ca/products/draeger-4042056). Its [contact page](https://www.supplynow.ca/pages/contact-us) gives **4190 Steeles Avenue West, Unit B5, Vaughan, ON** and **905-725-2266**, with a contact form.

The reviewed evidence establishes product listings and a Canadian business contact. It does not verify manufacturer-authorized dealer status, model-specific current stock, a wholesale appointment or Canada-wide delivery commitments. A business-account registration is not sufficient evidence of resale rights. The absence of verified authorization here is a research limitation, not a finding that the company lacks authorization.

**Commercial enquiry to resolve:** trade/resale terms, the authorized supply chain and manufacturer warranty, current orderability of the exact variants, cylinder/configuration details, shipping restrictions and returns. Use as a comparison or supplementary sourcing route after manufacturer-channel clarification.

## Information to obtain before a commercial arrangement

These are proposed diligence questions, not messages already sent:

- Can Superclean Canada Inc., trading through EEBD.ca, resell the named models in Canada? What geography, market, online-listing and trademark permissions would the agreement cover?
- Which exact manufacturer part numbers and configurations would be supplied? Request current instructions, applicable approval documents, product traceability and any model/serial-specific safety notices. Do not substitute M-20, M-20.2, a training unit or a different Saver variant based on a similar product name.
- What are the written trade terms: minimum orders, currency, payment, returns, warranty, product age at delivery, freight responsibility and quoted lead time for each order?
- Who is authorized to provide model-specific familiarization, training, inspection or service? What Canadian locations and referral arrangements are actually available?
- May EEBD.ca use supplied photographs, logos and technical descriptions? Can the supplier provide approved English/French material and a process for keeping it current?

Keep supplier logos, authorization claims, stock promises and named service partnerships off the public website until the relevant relationship and publication permissions are confirmed. This shortlist does not change EEBD.ca’s current enquiry-only business status.

## Research limits

All evidence links above are primary manufacturer or supplier sources reviewed on the research date; some directory records are exposed in the search index rather than in the directory’s initial dynamic page. Catalogue and directory entries can change. Published prices and estimated delivery dates were deliberately not carried into this document because no order-specific quote was requested. Physical presence, brand authorization, resale terms and service authorization were evaluated separately; evidence for one was not treated as proof of the others.
