Reviewed 16 November 2026. These findings support informational model-enquiry pages. EEBD.ca has no confirmed supplier, trainer or service-provider arrangements. A manufacturer listing or Canadian distribution channel does not establish stock, a resale relationship, delivery dates, Canadian acceptance of a particular configuration, or authority for EEBD.ca to service a device.

## Ocenco M-20.2

The [current manufacturer product page](https://www.ocenco.com/products/m-202/) identifies compressed oxygen, a closed-circuit system, dimensions of **6.9 × 16.7 × 15.5 cm**, **1.0 kg as donned**, a **10-minute NIOSH** rating and **15-minute CE** rating. It lists NIOSH, CE/EN 13794 and MED Wheelmark approvals. Present the ratings separately; do not call it an unqualified “15-minute NIOSH device.”

The page also lists the separate **M20.2T trainer, part 940011**, stowage case **648101**, steel bulkhead rack **648061**, aluminium rack **648064**, and wire mounting bracket **648062**. These are useful identifiers for an accessory or familiarization enquiry; they are not confirmed EEBD.ca inventory or universally compatible accessories for other models.

The linked [instruction manual NH22050, Revision F](https://www.ocenco.com/media/1022/m-202-instruction-manual_ocenco-inc.pdf) contains older CE/EN 400 and weight figures (900 g for the apparatus) that differ from the current product page. Do not combine that table with the current specifications or silently resolve the discrepancy. Request the applicable current instructions and certificate for the actual model/label before procurement. Do not turn its conditional service-life language into an unconditional warranty or replacement schedule.

The manufacturer's linked [ABS MED Module B certificate 07-280652-6-EC](https://www.ocenco.com/media/xbyl0jy0/m-202-med-abs-ec-type-examination-certificate-module-b-exp-10-23-30.pdf) identifies the M20.2 and references ISO 23269-1:2008 and EN 13794:2002 in its schedule. Module B alone is not proof that every offered unit has all required production-conformity documentation, and it is not evidence of Canadian approval for every application. Retain the certificate as a document to check against the proposed unit, not as a blanket Canadian compliance claim.

The [manufacturer distributor directory](https://www.ocenco.com/distributors/) lists **Viking Life-Saving Equipment** and **Survitec Group** under maritime/offshore distribution. It lists **Levitt Safety** and **Vallen** for Canada's mining market. These are channel leads; the directory does not prove a particular Canadian branch supplies a marine M-20.2 configuration or will enter a relationship with EEBD.ca.

The manufacturer's [product-information notices](https://www.ocenco.com/product-information/) include an **M20.3** notice dated June 13, 2023. That is a different model; do not apply it to M-20.2. No M-20.2-specific current recall was identified in the public sources reviewed. This is not a comprehensive clearance: check exact serials and current manufacturer notices before any actual supply.

Useful enquiry fields: existing model/approval-label wording; serial and manufacture date; unit quantity; stowage locations; vessel or workplace application; requested trainer/accessory identifiers; delivery city; relevant authority or surveyor requirements. Manufacturer documents establish specifications, while the eventual supplier must confirm the exact configuration, condition and available documentation.

## Interspiro Spiroscape: bar configurations

The [manufacturer Spiroscape EEBD (bar) page](https://interspiro.com/en-gb/products/emergency-escape/spiroscape-eebd-bar) describes a **15-minute**, open-circuit compressed-air escape device. Its standard cylinder is **3 L / 200 bar steel**; an alternative is **2 L / 300 bar composite**. The listed dimensions are **500 × 230 × 130 mm**. The respective listed weights are **4.8 kg with an empty steel cylinder** and **2.1 kg with an empty composite cylinder**. Preserve “empty cylinder”; those are not verified charged ready-for-use weights.

The manufacturer lists **EN 1146**, **ISO 23269-1**, the **PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425**, and **MED 2014/90/EU** for this family. Treat these as manufacturer-listed standards/approval references requiring confirmation for the actual part number and certificate. The bar family is distinct from the separately listed US/NIOSH Spiroscape; do not blend their specifications or approvals. Canadian acceptance and cylinder requirements must be established for the actual intended application.

The [official user-manual index](https://interspiro.com/en-gb/service-support/downloads/user-manuals) lists **95542O91 — Spiroscape User Manual (EN DE FR SE NL ES IT NO)**, document 95542 revision O, including English and French. Its [direct download](https://interspiro.com/Admin/Public/DWSDownload.aspx?File=%2FFiles%2FFiles%2FDownload%2FUser+manuals%2FFirefighting%2F95542O+-+Spiroscape%2F95542O91+-+Spiroscape+User+Manual+%28EN+DE+FR+SE+NL+ES+IT+NO%29.pdf) was exposed by the manufacturer index but the research browser could not parse the application/octet-stream response. Thus the manual's current contents were not independently inspected; do not claim otherwise. Link the index as the stable documentation route. Part numbers and filled/empty delivery configurations require confirmation; no exact orderable SKU was established from the rendered page.

The [manufacturer contact directory](https://interspiro.com/en-gb/contact-us) includes Canadian channels such as **Northwest Safework Solutions (Langley, BC)**, **Safety Source Industrial (Dartmouth, NS)**, **Canadian Safety Equipment (Mississauga, ON)**, **CSE Incendie et Sécurité (Saint-Laurent, QC)** and **Boivin & Gauvin (Trois-Rivières, QC)**. These are brand-level listings, not confirmation of Spiroscape stock, model-specific support or an EEBD.ca relationship.

No Spiroscape-specific current recall was identified in the [manufacturer important-notices index](https://interspiro.com/en-gb/service-support/downloads/important-notices). That finding does not establish that every cylinder, valve or serial is unaffected by other applicable notices. Obtain the exact unit and cylinder information for a current check.

Useful enquiry fields: 3 L/200 bar steel or 2 L/300 bar composite; exact existing part/serial numbers; required approval documentation; filled or empty delivery requirement; cylinder and storage constraints; quantity and delivery port; current training equipment. Do not imply that a local directory listing guarantees cylinder filling, maintenance or Canadian acceptance.

## Dräger Saver CF10 N

Use the exact **CF10 N, part 4042056**. The [current manufacturer product information DMC-109821, 26.05-1, ©2026](https://www.draeger.com/Content/Documents/Products/Saver-CF-NIOSH-pi-109821-en-us.pdf) lists:

- Nominal duration **10 minutes**; compressed-air constant-flow escape hood.
- Dimensions **240 × 180 × 440 mm (H × D × L)** and weight **5.12 kg**.
- Cylinder charging pressure **3,000 psi**; air flow **35–37 L/min**.
- Operating temperature **−15 to +60 °C**; storage **−15 to +25 °C**.
- **NIOSH 42 CFR Part 84 Self-Contained Escape Respirator (SCER)** approval description.

The same current brochure lists **CF5 N, part 4042055**, nominal **5 minutes**, **240 × 180 × 320 mm**, **3.98 kg**, with the same stated pressure/flow/temperature ranges and NIOSH approval. This is a distinct variant and should not be presented as interchangeable with a 10-minute marine requirement. It can be documented internally without adding a fourth website page.

The [Canadian family product webpage](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_ca/Products/Saver-CF-N-NIOSH) still contains broad 5/10/15-minute and ABS/SOLAS/Transport Canada wording, while stating that the 15-minute version is temporarily unavailable. The current 2026 brochure contains only 5- and 10-minute variants and an exact-model NIOSH table. Prefer its exact CF10 N specifications, and obtain model-specific marine/Canadian certificates rather than copying broad family approval claims.

The older [Saver CF NIOSH instructions 3362378, Edition 10, January 2020](https://www.draeger.com/Content/Documents/Products/saver-cf-niosh-ifu-3362378-en-us.pdf) describe CF5 N with a 1 L aluminium cylinder, CF10 N with a 2 L aluminium cylinder and CF15 N with a 3 L hoop-wrapped cylinder. They say to contact Dräger for marine-approval details. That older manual does not establish current CF15 N availability or supersede the 2026 brochure. Obtain the applicable current instructions and approval label for a proposed unit.

The Canadian product page and current brochure identify **Draeger Safety Canada Ltd., Mississauga, Ontario**, providing a direct Canadian manufacturer channel. They do not establish any EEBD.ca supply or service arrangement.

### Unresolved 2026 Saver CF safety alert

[ABS's safety recall notice dated August 18, 2026](https://ww2.eagle.org/en/rules-and-resources/regulatory-updates/regulatory-news-articles/safety-recall-draeger-saver-cf-emergency-escape-breathing-devices-eebds.html) identifies certain **Saver CF** units with serial numbers **BSUD-0001 through BSUK-9999**, manufactured **April–September 2025**. It describes insufficient airflow and possible elevated carbon dioxide within the hood, and directs owners to Dräger's notice titled “Saver CF Emergency Escape Breathing Device – Insufficient Flow.”

ABS is a primary classification-body source, but the original manufacturer notice was not located in the public manufacturer sources reviewed. **Do not infer that CF10 N is either affected or cleared from the family name alone.** Ask Dräger to establish applicability and required action for the actual serial number before any supply or service commitment. A page may identify the ABS notice as a document requiring model/serial verification; it must not announce that a product is recall-free or that a remedy has been completed.

Useful enquiry fields: full model and part number; serial/manufacture date; current approval-label photograph; required duration; application and authority requirements; quantity; delivery city; confirmation that applicable safety notices have been resolved. Do not request a safety-critical operational test from a website visitor.

## Real imagery and reuse decision

### Cleared route: historical M-20.2 photograph in CDC/NIOSH report

The [CDC Stacks record for PPE CASE P2018-0105](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/137510) explicitly marks the report **Public Domain**. **Figure 1 on PDF page 3** of the [report PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/137510/cdc_137510_DS1.pdf) identifies the pictured device as **Ocenco M20.2 EEBD**. It shows the device/case without an identifiable person or uniform. No separate third-party photograph restriction was identified in the report. This is a concrete source for a real historical reference image; it is not evidence of EEBD.ca stock or the appearance of a currently offered batch.

Follow [CDC's agency-materials terms](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html): credit the agency, clearly state non-endorsement, preserve the substantive depiction, say the original is freely available from CDC and link it. Do not use CDC/HHS logos or imply government endorsement. Extract the photograph faithfully rather than generating missing device components or presenting the 2018 report as a 2026 product assessment.

Suggested caption: **“Historical Ocenco M-20.2 reference photograph. Source: CDC/NIOSH, PPE CASE P2018-0105, Figure 1 (2018), public domain. Original report available free from CDC. Use does not imply endorsement of EEBD.ca or its products by CDC, HHS or the U.S. Government.”** Link “original report” to the CDC record. Translate the caption and non-endorsement on the French page. Keep it readable near the image.

## Publication boundaries

No outreach, supplier verification or licensing request was performed. Publish exact-model educational references and a tailored enquiry form, with manufacturer/document links. State availability and configuration are to be confirmed. Do not add invented stock levels, prices, delivery promises, approval badges, service authorization, partner logos or declarations that a model meets all Canadian requirements. Preserve unresolved certificate, manual-version and recall questions in the procurement handoff. Recheck current manufacturer documentation before any real quotation or order.
